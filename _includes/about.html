About

I am a project manager and developer based in Hudson, New York, with an MBA from the University of New Hampshire and a PMP certification. Throughout my career, I have managed multi-million dollar portfolios, built PMOs, and led AI adoption efforts.

Having worked across both small agile shops and massive organizations, including Hinge, Cameo, Profound Logic, and the New York State Office of the Attorney General, I'm at home wherever there are great people doing amazing things.

I think the value of project and program management is all about communication, unblocking people, and enabling great work to be done.
